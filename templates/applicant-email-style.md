## Hard rules the drafter must follow

- Do not claim I have read a paper when only its abstract was available.
- Do not invent qualifications, results, or funding.
- Do not promise anything on my behalf.
- Do not use generic flattery.
- Ask about student availability rather than asserting an opening exists.
- Follow the recipient's own stated contact instructions; surface any conflict.
